Screening questions.

The practical details before you start.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. PRISM is an experimental screening tool. Its output does not replace a formal assessment by a qualified professional.

Which images can I upload?

Choose one PNG, JPEG, WebP, or BMP image up to 25 MiB. Use a clear, evenly lit image of the handwriting you want to review.

What does the score mean?

The score summarizes the model’s screening signal for the image. It is not a model accuracy measure or a clinical diagnosis.

What does fold disagreement mean?

The ensemble models can return different scores. The displayed range describes those differences, rather than a clinical confidence interval.

Are my handwriting images saved?

Saving a result is a separate action. Image retention is optional and starts off. Unsaved results are lost on reload, closing the app, or signing out.

Why does a PDF take time?

The report service screens the original image again before creating the PDF. A report is a new analysis and may differ from a saved result.
